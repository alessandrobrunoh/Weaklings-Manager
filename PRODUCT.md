# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Weaklings Manager serves Albion Online guild members and, primarily, officers and administrators who need to review guild activity, manage events, loot splits, bank operations, regear requests, compositions, permissions, and member information efficiently. Members also use the product to understand their status and complete the actions available to their role.

## Product Purpose

Provide a fast, reliable operational workspace for managing a guild. Every screen should make the current state clear, surface the next useful action, and support repeat tasks without visual friction. Success means officers can make decisions and execute routine operations quickly, while members can find their information without needing training.

## Brand Personality

Precise, capable, calm. The product should feel like a focused command instrument: dense enough for frequent operators, disciplined rather than flashy, and immediately trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative gaming dashboards, fantasy-themed ornament, oversized marketing-card grids, rainbow status color, bulky controls, and novelty interactions. Do not reproduce Linear branding, copy, or proprietary UI assets. Take inspiration only from its compact operational clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current task ahead of decoration.
2. Make repeated operations fast through consistent structures and predictable controls.
3. Use visual hierarchy and restraint to make important guild signals immediately legible.
4. Treat density as a usability tool, while preserving readable touch targets and accessible focus states.
5. Use color as functional state and direction, never as ambient noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard-only operation, highly visible focus indicators, readable contrast in both themes, reduced motion preferences, clear non-color status cues, and responsive layouts for members using smaller devices.
